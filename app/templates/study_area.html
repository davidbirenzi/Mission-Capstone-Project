Study area

Nyandungu is one restored wetland in Kigali, about 122 hectares, between Gasabo and the Kanombe side of the city. Restoration works ran from September 2018 to 2021. The eco-park opened on 8 July 2022.

Why this site can defend the result

The change is documented, it sits inside the Sentinel-2 years, and it stayed in place after the park opened. Ponds, channels, and planted vegetation do not appear for one season and then vanish. A method that is right here should light up that restored footprint across successive dates, and stay quiet on a single wet-season spike.

The persistent change on this site is recovery. In this Sentinel-2 record the clear signal is vegetation: median NDVI inside the park rises and then stays higher, while the land outside the park does not. The same detector is the one the proposal uses for a lasting departure from normal conditions.
