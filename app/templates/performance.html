How to read this

The reference is a sustained NDVI increase inside the restored park. That is the direction of the documented change at Nyandungu over this satellite record. The MLP never sees that label while it trains. It only sees pre-2018 NDVI. The comparison asks whether a seasonal residual that keeps recurring is closer to that reference than one pair of dates.

Recall is the share of sustained park greening that the detector finds. Precision is lower because some persistent greening also occurs outside the park, and those pixels are not counted as reference positives. Accuracy stays high because most of the window is stable.
