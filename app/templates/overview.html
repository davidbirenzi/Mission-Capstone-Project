What this phase demonstrates

  1. A local Sentinel-2 time series for Nyandungu Urban Wetland Eco-Tourism Park, with cloud masking and NDVI and NDWI.
  2. A multilayer perceptron that learns expected NDVI from the season and from each pixel’s pre-restoration level.
  3. A persistence rule: a pixel is kept only when the deviation continues across successive post-opening observations.
  4. A two-date NDVI baseline, scored on the same held-out pixels with precision, recall, F1, accuracy, and IoU.
  5. This web interface, which is the phase-1 deployment of those results.
