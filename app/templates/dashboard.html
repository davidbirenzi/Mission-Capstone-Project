How it works

  1. Learn normal: harmonic regression and an MLP learn expected NDVI for every pixel from the normal period (season + the pixel's own normal levels).
  2. Detect deviation: observed − expected NDVI (the residual) is tracked through the monitoring period.
  3. Require persistence: a change is reported only when the deviation stays large for consecutive clear observations — with onset date, duration, magnitude and confidence.
  4. Type & quantify: NDVI/MNDWI/NDMI/BSI shifts classify the change (vegetation gain/loss, water loss, built-up encroachment…); connected areas become map polygons with hectares.
  5. Evaluate honestly: spatially held-out blocks, precision/recall/F1/accuracy/IoU, against a conventional two-date baseline — with limitations stated, not hidden.
