How to read these numbers

Precision = of the pixels we flagged, how many the reference agrees with. Recall = of the reference change pixels, how many we caught. IoU = spatial overlap. Precision is usually the hardest here: genuine lasting change outside a mapped reference (e.g. greening just outside a park boundary) counts against precision even when it is real — read the control-ring row and the maps alongside the scores, and see the limitations section of the methods note.
